Where to look: what changed on the screens

A short tour of the six places in the app where the auditor's rule now shows, with what was there before and what is there now. Use it to demonstrate the change to the client.

The rule in one line. Anything that has reached the ledger is never deleted. It is cancelled, and its accounting entries are reversed by a linked mirror entry. Only drafts are still deleted.

1. Payment form: the Actions menu

Accounting → Payments (outgoing or incoming) → open any sent or received payment → Actions button (إجراءات)
Before
Reset payment (إعادة تعيين الدفعة) deleted the accounting transactions outright.
Now
A new item إلغاء الدفعة (Void) sits below Reset. Reset no longer deletes anything: it writes a reversing entry and returns the payment to draft. Void keeps the payment on file as "Voided", reverses its entries, cancels its bank row and asks for a reason.

What to show

  1. Open a sent payment. Click Actions → Transactions to show the posted entry.
  2. Run Reset. Status goes to "Not paid". Now open Accounting → Accounting Transactions and filter by the payment id: the original is still there, and a new row "Reversal of …" sits next to it with debit and credit swapped.
  3. Open a received payment and choose Void. The dialog explains what happens and will not let you confirm without a reason.

2. Payments list: the Delete button

Accounting → Payments → scroll the grid to the Actions column on the right
Before
Delete was active on every row, including sent and received payments.
Now
Delete is greyed out on any payment past draft. Hovering shows why: sent or received payments are voided from the payment screen instead. Drafts still delete normally.

3. Accounting transactions list: Reverse replaces Delete

Accounting → Organization GL Settings → Accounting Transactions (القيود المحاسبية)
Before
Every row had a red Delete button, posted or not.
Now
Posted rows show an amber عكس القيد (Reverse) button. Only unposted rows keep the red Delete. Reverse asks for a reason and writes a linked mirror entry; the original stays. Reversing the same entry twice is refused.
This screen needs the role deleteAcctgTrans to show the buttons at all. The dev user did not have it; it was assigned for the test.

4. Payroll run: a settled month cannot be wiped

Accounting → Invoices → Payroll Run (تشغيل الرواتب) → pick a month whose salary payment was already sent, e.g. July 2026 → "Delete existing invoices"
Before
The whole month was deleted, invoices and payments included, even after salaries were paid.
Now
Refused. The server names the sent payments and says to correct next month or by reversal. Nothing changes.

5. Invoice: reset refused once a payment is applied

Accounting → Invoices → open an invoice that a sent or received payment is applied to (e.g. INV115) → Actions → Reset
Before
Reset always went through, deleting the accounting entries and every payment application, and erasing the status history.
Now
Refused when a sent or received payment is applied, naming that payment. When no money has moved the reset still works, and the invoice's status history is kept with the reset recorded as one more line.

6. Sales request: reset refused once an instalment is received

Sales → Sales Requests → open an approved request with a received payment (e.g. 10904) → Actions → Reset Sales Request
Before
Reset wiped every customer payment and accounting entry generated at approval.
Now
Refused, naming every received payment, with the advice to void that payment first or cancel the request. This screen shows the server's message properly.

7. Employee advances: delete becomes cancel

Human Resources → Employee Advances (السلف) → Delete on an advance whose payment was sent
Before
The advance, its sent payment and all accounting entries were deleted.
Now
The dialog says nothing will be deleted. The advance stays in the list marked ملغاة (Cancelled), the payment becomes Voided and its entry is reversed.

Not yet visible: the period lock

Closing a month from Accounting → Organization GL Settings → Time Periods now locks it: nothing can be written into it and nothing reversed or deleted out of it. Monthly periods for 2025 and 2026 exist but none is closed yet, so there is nothing to show until the first month is closed. That is the last test.
