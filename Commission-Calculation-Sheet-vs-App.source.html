How Sales Commission Is Calculated

The Excel sheet vs. the ERP — what matches, what doesn't, and what actually affects money.
Golden Land · 7 September 2026

Every commission answers three questions in order:

  1. How much is earned?  Sale price × the person's percentage.
  2. How much is payable right now?  Depends on how much the customer has paid so far.
  3. How much after tax?  Brokers only. Internal staff have no tax deducted.

The sheet and the app agree on question 1. They differ on question 2. Only the app does question 3 at all.

Part A — Internal staff (sales rep & manager)

Question 1: how much is earned — both agree

commission = sale price × percentage

For unit A-104 in سوا: sale price 4,416,774. The rep gets 0.5%, the manager 0.25%.

But no one has ever been underpaid by this. The 50% only affects the preview line «المستحق الآن» on a commission that is still pending. The moment you press اعتماد, the app ignores the band completely and creates the payment for the full 100% — 22,084, the same as the sheet. So this is a screen-vs-sheet mismatch, not a money mismatch.

Part B — Brokers: the tax steps (app only)

The Excel sheet has no broker tax calculation anywhere — it only covers internal staff. Everything below exists only in the app.

Two different taxes, two separate checkboxes

TaxWhat it meansTurned off by
VAT (ض.ق.م) 14% Already inside the broker's gross amount, so it is backed out first. إعفاء من ضريبة القيمة المضافة
Withholding (ض.استقطاع) 5% Charged on the amount left after VAT is removed. إعفاء من ضريبة الاستقطاع

The two are independent. A broker can be exempt from VAT and still pay withholding tax. Ticking one box does not switch off the other.

Worked example — the broker on unit A-104

  1. Gross: 4,416,774 × 3.5% = 154,587
  2. Strip out the VAT that is already inside it:
    154,587 ÷ 1.14 = 135,603
    so the VAT portion is 154,587 − 135,603 = 18,984
  3. Withholding is 5% of that stripped amount:
    135,603 × 5% = 6,780
  4. Net paid to the broker:
    154,587 − 18,984 − 6,780 = 128,823

If the broker is VAT-exempt

Step 2 is skipped — there is no VAT inside the amount, so the gross is the base. Withholding is then charged on the full gross:

154,587 − 0 − (154,587 × 5%) = 146,858

This is the part that was wrong before 25 August: a VAT exemption used to switch off the withholding tax too, so the broker was paid the whole 154,587.

The broker's own rep and manager

They are individuals, not companies — no VAT is involved. Only withholding applies, on the full amount:

44,168 − (44,168 × 5%) = 41,959

The one open decision The app currently deducts VAT from what the broker is paid, so the company keeps it. The alternative — the more usual treatment — is to pay the broker the full VAT-inclusive amount and let them remit the VAT themselves, deducting only withholding. On unit A-104 that is the difference between paying the broker 128,823 and 147,807. Worth confirming with whoever owns the tax treatment.
