1. Summary

The 6 September report counted 13 delete paths. A full sweep of the Application layer for any statement that removes AcctgTrans, AcctgTransEntry, FinAccountTrans or Payment rows found six more, bringing the register to 19. Each was read in full, its precondition and its blast radius recorded, and its exposure measured against the live database.

Classification is by what the artifact is allowed to become, not by what the code does today. Seven sites must become reversals, six must become voids, three keep a physical delete behind a guard, two are edit paths that must refuse once posted, and one is dead code to retire. No site is correct as written. The one handler that already gets it right, DeleteParty, refuses when ledger rows exist and is the pattern every guarded delete copies.

2. The rule applied

The auditor's instruction, "deletes should be marked as deleted, not removed", is applied with the boundary agreed in the September report: it governs anything that has ever touched the general ledger, plus anything referenced by something that has. Drafts that never posted may still be removed outright. Four operations follow from that, and every site below is assigned exactly one, sometimes with a second for its draft state.

OperationPreconditionWhat happens to the ledgerWhat happens to the artifact
EditNothing posted yetNothingChanged in place
ReversePosted, period open, artifact will be corrected and re-postedNew contra transaction, opposite signs, dated in the open period, linked to the original. Both visible.Returns to its editable status
VoidPosted, artifact should never have existedSame reversal as aboveStatus → *_CANCELLED / PMNT_VOID. Row stays, excluded from operational lists.
DeleteNever posted, nothing references itNothing to doPhysically removed. Refused otherwise.

4. The register

Each site records the entry point, what the code refuses today, what it removes, the live exposure, the classification, and the change that classification implies. A flagged line is a defect independent of the auditor's finding.

Payments

1ResetPayment Reverse

Entry
POST /payments/reset/{id} · Payment form action "إعادة تعيين الدفعة", gated by Can perform="Reset_Payment"
Refuses today
Status PMNT_VOIDED, PMNT_CANCELLED, PMNT_CONFIRMED. PMNT_VOIDED is not a seeded status; the seeded id is PMNT_VOID, so a voided payment is not actually refused.
Removes
Payment applications, then every transaction and entry keyed by the payment id. Status back to PMNT_NOT_PAID, override account cleared.
Leaves behind
The FinAccountTrans row is not touched, which is correct: it is created with the payment at draft time and belongs to the payment, not to its posting. Invoices the payment had settled stay INVOICE_PAID. Transactions anchored only by fin-account-trans id are missed.
Exposure
2,978 sent or received payments can be reset.
Becomes
Posted → reversal linked to the original, applications removed and their invoices returned to INVOICE_READY, status PMNT_NOT_PAID. The bank row stays with the payment. Draft → nothing to do. Refused in a closed period.

2DeletePayment Void Delete while draft

Entry
DELETE /payments/{id}
Refuses today
Linked to a sales request, an employee advance, a project certificate, or applied to an invoice. No status guard: a PMNT_SENT payment with bank and ledger rows is deleted whole.
Removes
Transactions and entries by payment id, fin-account rows, then the payment.
Becomes
Draft with no ledger or bank rows → physical delete, guarded the DeleteParty way. Anything else → PMNT_VOID plus reversal, fin-account row to FINACT_TRNS_CANCELED. The OFBiz port PaymentHelperService.VoidPayment already does most of this and is the primitive to promote (section 6).

3DeleteEmployeeAdvance Void Delete while draft

Entry
DELETE /humanresources/{id}?dropPayment=
Refuses today
Advance already on a payroll invoice, or any deduction started.
Removes
Schedules, the advance, and, when its payment is draft or DropPayment is passed, the payment with its transactions and bank row. The DropPayment override deletes a disbursed payment.
Exposure
288 advances, 287 of them with a payment beyond draft.
Becomes
Payment disbursed → advance ADVANCE_CANCELLED and the payment voided through the same primitive as site 2. Payment still draft → delete both. The override goes.

4UpdatePayment → DeletePostdatedChequeAccountingTransaction Reverse and re-post

Entry
PUT /payments (edit form) · GeneralLedgerService.DeletePostdatedChequeAccountingTransaction
Trigger
Editing a cheque's amount, account or details deletes the CHECK_ISSUED transaction and creates a fresh one; clearing the bank account removes the fin-account row. The method's own comment names reversal as "Option B, safer audit trail".
Becomes
Reverse the existing transaction, then post the new one, so the edit is visible as three linked rows. If the auditor accepts the pragmatic pre-send exception from the September report, this path can stay as is while the payment is PMNT_NOT_PAID and the period is open. Needs the written agreement either way.

5CompleteSalesOrderPayments · UpdateOrApproveOrderPayments Delete, guarded

Entry
Sales-order payment grid, IsPaymentDeleted flag on a row
Removes
The payment, its order payment preference and order status rows. Does not look at the ledger at all.
Exposure
Low for this client: the sales-order flow is barely used, and foreign keys would stop the delete if a transaction existed. It is listed because the flag is an unguarded delete of a financial artifact.
Becomes
Keep the delete, add the DeleteParty refusal when any transaction or fin-account row references the payment.

Payroll

6ResetInvoice Reverse

Entry
POST /invoices/reset/{id} · Invoice display form and accounting menu
Refuses today
Anything but INVOICE_READY.
Removes
Status history other than in-process, all transactions by invoice id, and every payment application on the invoice. Applications are deleted without touching the payments on the other side, so a sent payment silently becomes unapplied. The status history is itself audit evidence and should not be erased.
Exposure
550 invoice-anchored posted transactions. All 158 payroll invoices are posted, because the batch sets them INVOICE_READY at generation.
Becomes
Refuse when any application points at a payment beyond draft. Otherwise reverse the accrual, set INVOICE_IN_PROCESS, keep the status history. Under the pragmatic policy (before payment sent, period open) unpost-edit-repost may be allowed instead; that is the one decision to get from the auditor in writing.

7DeletePayrollInvoices Delete, guarded

Entry
POST /invoices/deletePayrollInvoices · Payroll run screen
Refuses today
Nothing.
Removes
The whole month for the organisation: invoices, items, roles, attributes, status history and their transactions; the aggregate payments addressed to the staff party with their transactions, bank rows and group memberships; advances and schedules put back to scheduled.
Becomes
The re-run window is legitimate, so the delete stays, but only while every payroll payment of the month is PMNT_NOT_PAID and the period is open. Once anything has been sent, refuse; corrections go through site 6 per invoice, or the next run.

8BatchCreatePayrollInvoices → DeletePayrollInvoicesSafelyAsync Delete, guarded

Entry
POST /invoices/batchCreatePayrollInvoices, the re-run branch
Removes
Same wipe as site 7, scoped to the employees in the request. The payment deletion is not scoped: re-running for a subset of employees deletes every staff-party payment for the month.
Becomes
Same guard as site 7, plus scope the payment deletion to the run being replaced. Do not re-run a month after this guard trips; the September report's warning about June stands.

5. Defects surfaced on the way

These are not part of the auditor's finding but were confirmed while reading the sites, and two of them change how step 2 is built.

  1. Status id mismatch. ResetPayment guards on PMNT_VOIDED; the seeded status and the void routine both use PMNT_VOID. One of them is wrong, and today the guard never fires. Settle on PMNT_VOID, which is the OFBiz id.
  2. Bank rows on draft payments are by design, not a leak. 90 fin-account rows sit on payments in PMNT_NOT_PAID. Checked on 14 September: the bank row is created together with a cheque payment at draft time (88 of the 206 draft cheque payments carry one), so Reset must leave it alone and only Void cancels it. The earlier reading of these rows as a ResetPayment leak was wrong and no cleanup is needed.
  3. The commented-out guard. DeleteAcctgTrans carries the posted check as a comment. Whoever removed it made the decision the auditor is now reversing.
  4. No orphans, by luck. Zero transactions point at a missing payment or invoice. Foreign keys, not the handlers, are what has kept the ledger internally consistent.
  5. Nothing has ever been closed. Six time periods, none closed, unchanged since the September report.

Whether the pragmatic exception stands: within an open period, before any payment is sent, may an invoice accrual be unposted, edited and re-posted rather than reversed? That agreement is the input to step 2.

7. How this was verified

Every handler in the register was read in full at master @ eeb49e0e, not sampled. The sweep that extended the list from 13 to 19 searched the Application layer for any ExecuteDeleteAsync, RemoveRange or Remove applied to the four financial tables, including the multi-line form where the table and the delete sit on different lines. Entry points were traced to their controller routes; frontend gating was checked for the payment reset. Counts were read from the local database with the queries below.

-- posted transactions by anchor
SELECT COUNT(*), SUM(IS_POSTED='Y') FROM ACCTG_TRANS WHERE PAYMENT_ID IS NOT NULL;      -- 3351 / 3351
SELECT COUNT(*), SUM(IS_POSTED='Y') FROM ACCTG_TRANS WHERE WORK_EFFORT_ID IS NOT NULL;  -- 1767 / 1767
SELECT COUNT(*), SUM(IS_POSTED='Y') FROM ACCTG_TRANS WHERE SALES_REQUEST_ID IS NOT NULL;-- 952 / 952
SELECT COUNT(*), SUM(IS_POSTED='Y') FROM ACCTG_TRANS WHERE INVOICE_ID IS NOT NULL;      -- 550 / 550

-- the reset-payment leak
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM FIN_ACCOUNT_TRANS f JOIN PAYMENT p USING (PAYMENT_ID)
WHERE p.STATUS_ID = 'PMNT_NOT_PAID';                                                    -- 90

-- seeded void status (not PMNT_VOIDED)
SELECT STATUS_ID FROM STATUS_ITEM WHERE STATUS_TYPE_ID='PMNT_STATUS' AND STATUS_ID LIKE '%VOID%'; -- PMNT_VOID
