The broker commission problem

Explained as simply as possible · 7 September 2026

The short version: when we pay a broker, sometimes we hand them the tax money and sometimes we keep it. It depends on one thing — did the broker give us their tax invoice?

Our app had no box to record that answer, so it had to guess. It guessed right for some commissions and wrong for others.

1. Two taxes, and they behave differently

Say we owe a broker 104,800 for selling an apartment. That number has two taxes wrapped up in it.

The 14% tax (ضريبة القيمة المضافة) — this money is not ours and not really the broker's. It belongs to the government. The only question is who hands it over.

The 5% tax (ضريبة الاستقطاع) — we always keep this and give it to the government ourselves. Every time. No exceptions. This one is easy.

2. So the only real question is about the 14%

Did the broker give us their tax invoice? (رفع الفاتورة الضريبية)

Yes, they gave it to us

تم رفعها

Then we hand them the 14% too. They will pass it to the government themselves.

No, not yet

Then we hold the 14% back until they bring it. We can't hand over tax money with no invoice.

We owe104,800
Take the 5%− 4,596
Hold the 14%− 12,870
They get 87,333

Same broker, same 104,800, and a difference of 12,870 — decided entirely by whether a piece of paper arrived.

3. What went wrong

The app had no box for that question. So it always applied the same answer to everybody.

First we told it "always hand over the 14%". That was right for some commissions and wrong for others. Then we told it "always hold the 14% back". Same problem, just reversed. There was no single answer that could be right, because the correct answer changes from one commission to the next.

That is why this was so confusing. Every time we checked a commission, the app looked either correct or broken depending on which one we happened to open. Nobody was misreading anything — the app simply could not tell the two situations apart.

4. What we did

We added the missing box to the screen: تم رفع الفاتورة الضريبية. Now the app asks instead of guessing, and it gets every commission right.
